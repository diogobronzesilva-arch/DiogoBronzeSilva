Cutline

A Mac app for turning long videos into short clips you can review, reframe and caption.

Platform
Mac with Apple silicon macOS 13.5 or later
Local Processing
Whisper on the Mac Up to 10 editorial clip suggestions
Video & Captions
1080 × 1920 MP4 9:16 framing · Editable SRT captions
Availability
Personal project No public download

From a long video to a shortlist

I built Cutline for podcasts, interviews and talks. Give it an authorised public video link and it transcribes the recording on the Mac with Whisper, then suggests up to ten short clips to preview.

The suggestions look for a clear opening, specific details, a steady rhythm and a conclusion. I review each clip for context and decide what is worth publishing.

Vertical video and editable captions

Cutline follows a face or subject to frame the clip vertically, then exports a 1080 × 1920 MP4.

It saves captions as a separate SRT file, so you can correct or style them in your editor while keeping a clean master video.

What runs on the Mac

Transcription and rendering run on the Mac, and working files stay there. The app runs without a subscription, an online account or an AI API key.

You need an internet connection to fetch a linked video and download the speech model on first use. Cutline is built for Apple silicon and macOS 13.5 or later.
