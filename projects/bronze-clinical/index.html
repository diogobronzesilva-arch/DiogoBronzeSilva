From the first search to an appointment

Patients need to find the right specialty, know who they’ll see, check which insurance plans the clinic accepts and see how to request an appointment. I bring the website, writing, photography and video together, with one point of contact from the first review to launch.

What a patient needs on their phone

We test the route a new patient takes from a search result to a booking request. The review looks at how quickly useful information appears, how easily people can identify a practitioner and whether they can check insurance and reach reception without unnecessary steps.

Photography, writing and a site built together

  • Clear clinical information. Pages for specialties and practitioners, an explanation of the first appointment, searchable insurance information and a direct booking route.
  • Photographs and video. We adapt existing material where it fits and can photograph the team and premises or film new introductions on site. We test how video behaves on mobile.
  • Web development. We measure mobile performance, connect existing booking tools or patient portals, and structure clinic and practitioner information so search engines can read it.
  • Portuguese healthcare requirements. We review how the site presents the clinic’s ERS registration, clinical director, professional registration details (OM or OMD) and Livro de Reclamações Eletrónico, alongside the advertising requirements in Decree-Law 238/2015.

A full website or one specialty to start

The full project covers the review, writing, visual direction, development and launch. A clinic can also start with one priority specialty, including a dedicated page, copy approved by the clinic and a direct route to reception.

The clinic owns the domain, code and visual files after launch. Ongoing support is optional. Introductory terms are available for the first clinics to work with Bronze Clinical.

How the project works

  • Review. Start with a five-page technical review with mobile performance measurements, screenshots of the patient journey and clear priorities. The review also looks at how the current site presents regulatory and privacy information.
  • Gather. A 30–45-minute interview with the clinical director gives us enough to draft the copy for the clinic’s review. If the clinic needs new photography or video, we plan one day on site.
  • Launch. A single-site clinic typically takes about 15 days. We agree a phased schedule for multi-site clinics and check the domain and email records before the move.

Frequently asked questions

Can we keep our existing photographs and video?

Yes. We review your existing material and adapt what works for the new site. New photography or video is scoped only where the clinic needs it.

Can the site use our booking software and host our video?

We can connect the site to your existing booking software or patient portal and include clinic and practitioner videos. We test playback and the booking route on mobile.

What happens to our clinic’s email and existing web addresses?

We document your DNS records, including the records used for email, before making changes. We preserve existing page addresses where practical and add redirects where addresses change.

Who owns the website after launch?

The clinic owns the domain, code, content and visual files. Ongoing technical and editorial support is optional.
