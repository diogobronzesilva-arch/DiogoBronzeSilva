VitaChart

A private fertility diary, built from family experience.

01

Why I started building it

For years, my wife and I have charted her cycle using standardized fertility observations with the guidance of a FertilityCare practitioner and a NaProTechnology doctor. VitaChart grew out of that part of our family life and my interest in women’s health and natural fertility.

I wanted a simple place to keep daily observations, see them together and prepare the questions we take to consultations—supporting both Standardized Code charting (Creighton / NaPro style, Peak Day P) and Vulvar Sensation & Peak charting (Billings / BOM style, Peak Day × and Basic Infertile Pattern) while preserving the original observations. Changing the selected method does not make incomplete observations clinically equivalent.

02

A record for the next consultation

The diary brings together observations of sensations while walking or wiping, cervical mucus characteristics, bleeding, basal body temperature (with 3-over-6 thermal shift detection), hormone tests (LH, PdG, HCG) and consultation questions.

Charts use 35 days per page in A4 Landscape PDF format and include consultation notes, using the selected method’s Peak Day marker: P for Creighton or × for Billings. Records can also be exported as CSV / Excel spreadsheets or local JSON backups. Automated summaries remain under validation and need review with an instructor or doctor. VitaChart does not diagnose conditions or replace instruction in the method.

03

Keeping the journal local

A fertility diary holds intimate information. I wanted to keep that record on the device and make daily recording work offline, without requiring an online account. That is how the current version works:

  • Local records: observations, cycle history and the optional profile are stored 100% locally on the device.
  • No advertising or third-party analytics: the app does not include advertising or analytics SDKs.
  • Exports you control: A4 PDF reports, CSV / Excel tables and JSON backups leave the app only when you choose to save or share them. The backup is not encrypted by the app.
  • Couple sharing is future work: remote synchronisation and encrypted pairing are not available in the current version.
04

What is available today

Daily recording uses a grid of 12 cards and supports 4 languages (English, Portuguese, Spanish and French, with automatic device language detection and English fallback). The journal includes optional local reminders, discreet mode for marital union markers, a profile photo processed on the device, consultation notes and backup and restore points. Android, iPhone and the installable web version share a React and TypeScript codebase.

  • Availability: Android and iPhone versions are in private testing. Store release is in preparation; there is no public download yet.
