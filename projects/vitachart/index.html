VitaChart

A private fertility diary with separate ways to record, chart and share observations for Creighton and Billings.

The web demo runs version 1.3.2. VitaChart for iPhone is available in participating App Store regions, including Brasil. The Android release is in testing.

Two ways to chart

Choose the method you use with your instructor. From version 1.3.2, each method has its own daily form, chart and export. Existing Creighton records stay in the Creighton history. Switching methods leaves the original entries intact.

Creighton Model

Record observations using standardised codes. The chart keeps the original codes and stamps, with P as the Peak Day marker.

The consultation PDF follows the Creighton chart and includes the observations and notes you recorded.

Try the Creighton demo →

Billings Ovulation Method

Record vulval sensation and visible discharge in your own words. The diary has a place to document your Basic Infertile Pattern (BIP) and changes you observe.

The Billings chart and PDF present those observations in their own layout, with × for a confirmed Peak.

Try the Billings demo →

Reports for your next consultation

Open Cycle in the method you recorded and review its chart before exporting the PDF. You can also export a CSV or Excel table for review and save a JSON backup to restore the diary later.

Review your observations and any suggested interpretation with your instructor or healthcare professional.

Keeping the journal local

The diary works offline and keeps observations, cycle history and your optional profile on the device. You can use it without creating an account.

  • No advertising or third-party analytics: the app does not include advertising or analytics SDKs.
  • Exports you control: A4 PDF reports, CSV / Excel tables and JSON backups leave the app only when you choose to save or share them. Standard JSON backups are readable as plain text. You can also export a password-protected backup.
  • Couple sharing is future work: remote synchronisation and encrypted pairing are not available in the current version.

Using the diary

VitaChart grew out of our family’s experience charting with the guidance of a FertilityCare practitioner and a NaProTechnology doctor. I wanted one place to keep daily observations and the questions we take to consultations.

The diary is available in English, Portuguese, Spanish and French. It includes optional reminders, a discreet mode for intimacy markers, consultation notes, and backup and restore.

References to Creighton Model, NaProTechnology, and Billings Ovulation Method are used strictly for descriptive compatibility.
  • Privacy: read the VitaChart Privacy Policy for storage, export and deletion details.
