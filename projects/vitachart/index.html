Why I started building it

VitaChart grew out of our family’s experience charting with the guidance of a FertilityCare practitioner and a NaProTechnology doctor. I wanted one place to keep daily observations and note the questions we take to consultations.

The diary supports Standardised Code charting (Creighton / NaPro style, with P for Peak Day) and Vulvar Sensation & Peak charting (Billings / BOM style, with × and Basic Infertile Pattern). It preserves the original observations when you change the charting method. Each method still needs its own observations.

A record for the next consultation

Record sensations while walking or wiping, cervical mucus, bleeding, basal body temperature and hormone test results (LH, PdG and HCG). The diary includes 3-over-6 thermal shift detection and a place for questions to take to your consultation.

A landscape A4 PDF shows 35 days per page, with consultation notes and the selected Peak Day marker. You can also export CSV or Excel files and save local JSON backups. Automated summaries remain under validation. Review them with an instructor or doctor. VitaChart is a diary and does not diagnose conditions or replace instruction in a charting method.

Keeping the journal local

The diary works offline and keeps observations, cycle history and your optional profile on the device. You can use it without creating an account.

  • No advertising or third-party analytics: the app does not include advertising or analytics SDKs.
  • Exports you control: A4 PDF reports, CSV / Excel tables and JSON backups leave the app only when you choose to save or share them. Standard JSON backups are readable as plain text. You can also export a password-protected backup.
  • Couple sharing is future work: remote synchronisation and encrypted pairing are not available in the current version.

Using the diary

The diary is available in English, Portuguese, Spanish and French. A set of 12 cards organises daily records. It includes optional reminders, a discreet mode for intimacy markers, a profile photo processed on the device, consultation notes, and backup and restore.

References to Creighton Model, NaProTechnology, and Billings Ovulation Method are used strictly for descriptive compatibility.
