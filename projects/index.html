Projects

Software, tools, and things I build.

Some projects begin at home, others in the working day. These are two I am building around daily observations, useful preparation and the details that need to be kept together.

01

Fertility diary · Android & iPhone · In development

VitaChart

A fertility diary that grew out of our family’s use of the Creighton Model, with a FertilityCare practitioner and a NaProTechnology doctor. I wanted a simple place to keep daily observations and the questions we take to consultations. VitaChart keeps that record on the device and prepares reports for review. Android and iPhone versions are in private testing; automated interpretations remain under validation.

02

Enterprise B2B SaaS · Operational AI

AI Sales Systems & Custom Workflows

Before a sales meeting, I need to understand the account, remember what each person has said and know what we agreed to do next. Sales Enablement and RevOps provide the shared foundations: playbooks, useful content, processes and a CRM. I build personal workflows around the details of the accounts I work with.

After more than a decade in software sales, including Pipedrive, deskbird and now Coverflex, I use AI for account research, meeting summaries, preparation and CRM follow-up. It helps me keep context and commitments together so I can give the conversation my attention. I review what it produces and remain responsible for what I use or send.
