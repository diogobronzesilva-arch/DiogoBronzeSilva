Questions about the projects

Are the AI sales workflows available as a software product?

I build and use them in my own sales work. The Deal Intelligence essay explains how I use them and where I keep human review.

Where can I see the Bronze Art photographs?

The public portfolio and session enquiries are on Bronze Art. Families receive a private link to their finished gallery.

Who is Bronze Clinical for?

Private medical and dental clinics in Portugal. A clinic can commission a complete website or start with a single specialty and a direct way for patients to request an appointment.

Where can I try VitaChart?

The VitaChart project page has the latest app availability. You can also try the web demo with fictional data.

Does Cutline work without the internet?

Transcription and rendering run on the Mac. Fetching a linked video and downloading the speech model on first use require a connection.
