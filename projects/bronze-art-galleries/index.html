Bronze Art

Documentary photography of family life, faith and what gets handed down.

Visit the photography portfolio ↗

Photographs for thirty years from now

I look for natural light, small gestures and the familiar details of a home or church. A photograph made at a family gathering or a sacrament may mean something different to the people in it thirty years later. I try to leave room for that.

The public portfolio holds my photography and stories. Each family receives a private link to a gallery I prepare for them.

A gallery for each family

I built Bronze Art’s gallery application so families can follow a finished story in chapters, download the full-resolution JPEGs and choose photographs for a printed album.

From a folder to a finished story

I start with a folder of edited JPEGs. Subfolders can become chapters, and I can review the cover, sequence, delivery note and download files before publishing. Uploads can resume after an interruption. I can also prepare a replacement while the current gallery remains available.

Downloads and the archive

Families download the JPEGs as I delivered them. Large collections are split into ZIP files assembled on request. Gallery access expires on a set date. I manage the internal archive separately, so keeping a collection in the archive does not extend the family’s link.
