Privacy Policy and Terms of Use — VitaChart

Last updated: 5 November 2026. This policy describes the operation of the local version of VitaChart and its terms of use.

1. Data on your device

VitaChart is a personal fertility observation journal. Individual use operates entirely without an account and without connection to any remote synchronisation service. The software in this version does not automatically transmit your journal to application servers. Your entries, profile, drafts, and restore points remain exclusively in local device storage. A recovery process for corrupted data may retain originals for review.

2. Data you can record

Name, date of birth, profile photo, walking and wiping sensations, mucus characteristics, bleeding intensity, marital relations, basal body temperature, medications, disturbances, test results, and notes. All recorded data is optional. The chosen photo is compressed locally on your device. We do not access the rest of your photo library through the system file picker.

3. Backups and reports

The JSON backup is a plain text file without proprietary VitaChart encryption. It includes your profile and historical data, even when discreet privacy mode hides relations from the chart view. The recovery archive file may contain rejected data and previous revisions. Store these backup files in a secure, private destination. The PDF/HTML clinical report contains observations for the selected cycle and respects your preference to hide relations. Exports are initiated solely by you and use the system share sheet or browser download. The chosen destination may be an external service governed by its own policies.

A local restore point is created each day when records exist, keeping up to eight recent points including manual ones. Optional access protection uses system authentication: fingerprint, facial recognition or the device passcode. VitaChart receives only the authentication result and does not access biometric data. The protection preference stays on this device and is not included in JSON backups. Android protection also blocks screenshots; iPhone protection hides the journal in the app switcher but allows screenshots while unlocked.

4. Temporary files and data reset

Native file exports may create a temporary private file to enable reading by the destination app you select. Settings → Reset data allows you to clear history or restart completely from scratch, with explicit confirmation. The reset includes drafts, recovery points, archived originals, and temporary export files. It does not delete backups or reports you have already saved or exported outside the app. If a reset operation fails, the app displays a warning.

5. Operating system and device transfers

Uninstalling the application normally removes its private application storage, but does not delete exported files, shared documents, or backups/transfers managed by your operating system. Backup and device transfer behaviours depend on the operating system and user settings. On Android, this version disables automated cloud backup via allowBackup=false; certain devices may still permit direct cable transfer between phones. The application cannot guarantee destruction of all copies upon uninstallation.

6. Permissions and privacy

The optional daily reminder uses purely local notifications. The system picker allows selecting a photo or backup file. This version contains no advertising, no advertising identifiers, no third-party tracking or analytics SDKs, and no remote push notification services. Discreet privacy mode hides relations on screen and in clinical reports; the JSON backup preserves original data for restoration.

7. Methodologies, clinical guidance, and institutional independence

VitaChart supports personal record-keeping and review of fertility biomarkers. The application is designed to be compatible with observational charting conventions, including the Creighton Model and NaProTechnology clinical protocols. VitaChart is not a medical device and does not provide clinical diagnoses. It does not replace formal instruction with a certified FertilityCare Practitioner or specialised medical care, nor does it guarantee efficacy in achieving or avoiding pregnancy. The terms “Creighton Model FertilityCare System” and “NaProTechnology” are trademarks of their respective owners and are mentioned strictly for informative purposes and recording compatibility.
