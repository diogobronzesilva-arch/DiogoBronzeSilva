03 · Invitation

Your experience would help.

I am inviting a small group of practitioners and testers to try VitaChart free of charge and tell me whether the daily record and consultation report are clear and useful. This web preview is read-only; the invited app test includes entering and editing records.

If you received a Google Play code, use the code in your invitation at Google Play ↗. Installation access is arranged through the invitation; this page does not provide a public download. If you need iPhone access, reply to your invitation to confirm the current testing options.

What I would like to hear

  • Is it clear how to record a day and distinguish a note from an observation of signs?
  • Can you read the chart easily, including missing observations and longer cycles?
  • Would the report help in a consultation? What is missing or difficult to read?
  • Which terms would you change? English, Spanish and French wording is open for professional review.

Reply to the invitation, or send feedback to Diogo. Fictional examples are enough to explain a problem.

About this independent project

VitaChart grew out of our family’s use of the Creighton Model with a FertilityCare practitioner and a NaProTechnology doctor. It keeps daily records on the device, without a mandatory online account. I am building VitaChart around a one-time purchase, without monthly or annual subscriptions. Invited testing is free.

VitaChart is in private testing. It is an independent diary, with no institutional endorsement or clinical certification. It does not diagnose conditions or replace instruction and professional guidance.
