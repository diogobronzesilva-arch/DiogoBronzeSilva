02

What the current project does

VitaChart grew out of our family’s use of the Creighton Model, with a FertilityCare practitioner and a NaProTechnology doctor. I wanted daily observations and the questions for our next consultation in one place. You can read that story on the project page.

  • Daily recording works offline and keeps the journal on the device, without a mandatory online account.
  • Reports use 35 days per page and can be printed or saved as a PDF for professional review.
  • The user controls exports and backups. JSON backups are not encrypted by the app; device security and care with shared files still matter.
  • Remote couple synchronisation and encrypted pairing remain future work.

03

Testing and availability

Android and iPhone versions are in private testing. There is no public download yet. Store pricing and release details need confirmation.

VitaChart is an independent diary. It is not an official release of the Saint Paul VI Institute, FCCA or AAFCP. It does not diagnose conditions or replace instruction and professional guidance. Technical tests do not establish clinical accuracy.

If you have a testing invitation with a Google Play promo code, redeem it in the Play Store under Profile → Payments & subscriptions → Redeem code, or at play.google.com/redeem.
