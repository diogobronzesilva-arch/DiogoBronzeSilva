What VitaChart is about

For over twelve years, my wife and I have used the Creighton Model to chart her natural fertility cycles, working hand in hand with a certified FertilityCare practitioner and a NaProTechnology medical doctor. As a software architect and a father of five, I created VitaChart out of real family practice and personal conviction.

Many couples find natural family planning methods powerful, empowering, and respectful of feminine physiology, but are frustrated by the software available today: recurring fees that feel exploitative, fragile cloud databases holding intimate health data, or generic period trackers that try to guess ovulation with blind statistical algorithms.

VitaChart replaces that complexity with quiet elegance: a dignified 12-card daily logging grid, offline-first local storage, and instant generation of a standardized 35-day consultation sheet ready for medical review.

Closed beta testing & practitioner feedback

VitaChart is currently in active private testing on Android (Google Play Closed Testing) and iPhone (TestFlight / local builds). Store release is scheduled following feedback from experienced practitioners and users.

  • Google Play Promo Codes: If you received an invitation promo code, you can redeem it directly in the Google Play Store under Profile → Payments & subscriptions → Redeem code, or at play.google.com/redeem to unlock VitaChart without charge.
  • Institutional Independence: VitaChart is an independent observational diary designed to assist couples. It is not an official release of the Saint Paul VI Institute, FCCA, or AAFCP, and does not replace certified instruction in natural methods.
  • Data Protection: Read the complete VitaChart Privacy Policy for full details on local device storage and export procedures.
