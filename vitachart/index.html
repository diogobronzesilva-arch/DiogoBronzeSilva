About this demo and the methods

This is the VitaChart 1.3.0 interface, with a fictional diary kept separate from personal records. The demo needs an internet connection the first time you open it. Your demo entries stay in this browser; please use fictional information only.

Billings and Creighton / NaPro retain their own options and notation. Interpretation depends on the signs recorded and should be reviewed with your instructor or healthcare professional. VitaChart is an independent charting tool, with no official certification from or affiliation with the Saint Paul VI Institute, FertilityCare Centers of America or WOOMB International.

Biometric locking is optional in the Android app and uses your phone’s authentication. Native authentication is not reproduced in the browser. Password-protected backups use AES-256 encryption, with verification before restore. Keep exported backups and their password in a private location.

Would you like to try the app on your phone? Get in touch. Invited testing is free.
