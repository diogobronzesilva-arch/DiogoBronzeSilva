Getting started

Four main sections

You can use the app on your own without an account. When you first open it, choose the charting convention that matches the method you follow.

Today

See where you are in your cycle and open your journal for the day.

Record

Enter the observations, temperature, symptoms and notes you want to keep.

Cycle

Review the chart, check your observations and prepare a report for your consultation.

Settings

Manage preferences, reminders, access protection and backups.

A simple daily routine

  1. Check the date and record only that day’s observations.
  2. Before leaving, look for the status Saved.
  3. Reopen the day and use Cycle to review your observations together.

For your consultation

Prepare a report

On iPhone and Android

In Cycle, select the cycle you want to review and tap Print / Save PDF. Check your observations and the setting that hides intercourse markers before sharing.

iPhone · In the report, tap Save / Share PDF and choose where to send or save it from the share menu.

Android · In the report, tap Print / Save PDF and choose Save as PDF in the print menu. You can then share the saved file.

In the web version

Open the cycle report and use your browser’s print or save as PDF option.

You can read, print or share the PDF. To restore your journal in the app, keep a separate backup file.

Keep your journal safe

Choose and save a backup

An exported backup keeps a copy of your data outside the app. Save the file in a private location you can access again.

Standard backup

Settings → Save a copy → Save backup

This creates a plain-text JSON file. It can include your profile and history, including information hidden on the chart by discreet mode.

Password-protected backup

Under Encrypted backup and integrity verification, fill in Encrypted backup password and select Export encrypted backup.

This option protects the exported backup with a password. Keep the password somewhere safe. You can still export a standard backup separately.

Restore your journal or switch phones

  1. Create a backup on the old phone and check that the file was saved.
  2. On the new phone, install VitaChart and open Settings → Save a copy → Load backup (Restore).
  3. Select the file and follow the instructions. You can use Verify backup to check the file before restoring it.
  4. Check your profile and entries from a few different dates before you stop using the old phone.

The app keeps up to eight recent local recovery points, including any you create manually. They stay on the same device. Save an external backup before switching phones, uninstalling the app or deleting data.

Offline use

Keep recording offline

Daily recording works offline in the installed app. Installing or updating the app requires an internet connection.

The web version also needs internet access the first time you open and set it up in your browser. Records stay on your device, without remote sync between phones.

Access and privacy

Protect access on your device

Optional access protection uses your device’s authentication. VitaChart receives the result of authentication without accessing your biometric data.

On iPhone, this hides the journal in the app switcher. On Android, it also prevents screenshots. Manage any exported backups separately.

Quick answers

Frequently asked questions

Do I need to create an account?

You can use VitaChart without an account. The journal stays on your device. The app includes no ads or third-party analytics.

Why can’t I see my records on my other phone?

The app does not sync records between devices. To move the journal, save a backup on the old phone and use Load backup (Restore) on the new one. Check your entries before you stop using the old phone.

Does discreet mode also hide information in a backup?

Discreet mode hides intercourse markers in the app and in reports. The JSON backup keeps the original data for recovery. Store the file somewhere private or use the encrypted option to protect an exported copy with a password.

Can I restore my journal from a PDF or spreadsheet?

Use the PDF to read, print or share a report. Export spreadsheet (CSV / Excel) lets you view your data in a table. To restore the journal in the app, use a compatible backup file.

My reminder didn’t appear. What should I check?

In your device settings, check that VitaChart can show notifications, then check the time set in the app. If the reminder still doesn’t appear, tell us the device model, system version, app version and reminder time.

I can’t find my records. How can I get help?

Keep your existing backups and describe what happened before the records disappeared. Tell us which device and app version you’re using and whether you have a backup or recovery points. Contact us before deleting data or reinstalling the app.

Need help?

Tell us which device and system version you use, the steps you followed and what happened. Use fictional data if you need to illustrate the issue.
