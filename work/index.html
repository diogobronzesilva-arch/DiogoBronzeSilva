Work · Senior Account Executive · Portugal, EMEA & UK&I

I sell software for a living.

I am Diogo Bronze Silva, a Senior Account Executive at Coverflex with over a decade in B2B SaaS across new business, expansion and customer growth in Portugal, EMEA and the UK&I. Alongside closing enterprise deals, I engineer Deal Intelligence workflows that bring account history, stakeholder concerns and deterministic financial calculations into a clear brief before every meeting. As generic pitching loses relevance, the Account Executive role is becoming that of a Sales Systems Architect: arrive better prepared, spend more time in the room, and help leaders make difficult decisions.

01

From product presenter to Sales Systems Architect

The era of the salesperson as an information broker or demo presenter is over. Buyers already have documentation, AI-generated comparisons and pricing signals before they agree to a call. Showing up simply to click through product features adds zero value. The future belongs to the Sales Systems Architect: someone who develops a deep, genuine interest in the client's industry, understands their unit economics, and synthesizes their commercial and operational structure in minutes.

Enterprise sales is not won by standardized RevOps playbooks. The customer's purchasing structure and the sales motion must be fine-tuned by the individual in the field.

02

Deal Intelligence, purchasing structures & deterministic systems

I build custom Deal Intelligence systems to run deals with engineering precision. In complex sales, language models synthesize unstructured context—procurement guidelines, call transcripts, stakeholder concerns and industry dynamics. But calculations, pricing governance and fiscal validation must remain deterministic. Automating this back-office synthesis does not eliminate the human element; it clears the operational noise so I can be fully present in the room, navigating the human friction that software can never solve.

Automate the backoffice ruthlessly to make room for what closes: trust, judgment and physical presence.

03

Enterprise FinTech at Coverflex

I'm a Senior Account Executive at Coverflex, working with Portuguese companies, founders, Finance directors and HR leadership. Compensation, flexible benefits and employee budgeting may look like software categories, but in practice they sit at the intersection of labor law, tax optimization and organizational trust. Navigating these purchasing structures requires understanding executive balance sheets and aligning diverse stakeholders around consequential fiscal decisions.

04

At Pipedrive, I built the foundational discipline of software sales across qualification, pipeline rigor and closing, helping secure its Series C and participating in its journey to unicorn status. At deskbird, I worked through the Series B round, expanding commercial footprint, retention and adoption across EMEA and the UK&I. At Coverflex, those foundations converge as the company scales through its Series B: architecting enterprise deals where technical systems, fiscal compliance and personal conviction matter in equal measure.

Startup environments are where I feel most at home: close to the customer, close to the code, and building systems that scale.
