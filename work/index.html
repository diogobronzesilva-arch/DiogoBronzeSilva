Work · Senior Account Executive · Portugal, EMEA & UK&I

I sell software for a living.

I’m a Senior Account Executive at Coverflex. I’ve spent more than a decade in B2B software sales, working on new business and customer growth in Portugal, EMEA and the UK & Ireland. I also build tools that help me prepare for complex sales conversations.

01

Helping buyers make a decision

Buyers often have product documentation, comparisons and a sense of pricing before we speak. Where I can help is in working through the decision with them: how their business works, who needs to be involved and what they need to know before buying.

02

How I use Deal Intelligence

I use language models to bring together call notes, procurement documents and questions from stakeholders. Pricing calculations and financial checks follow rules I can verify. I review the brief before using it to prepare for the next conversation.

03

Enterprise FinTech at Coverflex

At Coverflex, I work with founders, finance directors and HR teams at Portuguese companies. Much of that work is helping people with different priorities agree on how to manage compensation, benefits and employee budgets.

04

Pipedrive, deskbird and Coverflex

At Pipedrive, I learned to qualify opportunities, manage a pipeline and close software deals. At deskbird I worked across EMEA and the UK & Ireland on new business, adoption and retention. I draw on both experiences in my work at Coverflex.
