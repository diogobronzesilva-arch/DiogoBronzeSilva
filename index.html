Welcome to my home. Come in.

I live near Fátima, Portugal, with my wife, Sara, and our five children, which explains most of the noise in this house, and puts the rest of it in perspective.

I am Diogo Bronze Silva, a Senior Account Executive at Coverflex with over a decade in B2B SaaS sales across Portugal, EMEA and the UK&I. I build Deal Intelligence and AI workflows to take care of the routine so I can give my attention to the interesting part: helping founders, CFOs and HR leaders understand a problem and make a decision.

Around that, I build tools when a real problem needs one, photograph, read theology and philosophy, and write to think things through. This is where I keep all of it.

At Home

Sara, my wife, is the heroine of this story, though she would never describe herself that way. She is the centre of our home, and most of what is stable and beautiful in our life begins with her.

We chose a life with room for children, close to what matters to us. I work mostly from home, which often means breakfast with the children, seeing them again at lunchtime, and being part of ordinary days rather than hearing about them later. It is not always quiet, and it is rarely tidy. I would not trade it.

Faith holds it all together, and it runs through most of what I photograph and write.
